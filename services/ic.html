什么是顾问管理 IC

What contractor management means

以项目为基础提供服务的第三方或个人，不受雇主义务约束。适合与高技能专家按项目合作，也适合按业务节奏快速调整人员数量 —— 合同、发票、支付与成本归集由 Brio 统一承接。

Third parties or individuals engaged on a project basis, outside an employment relationship. A good fit for working with highly skilled specialists by project, and for flexing headcount with the business — contracts, invoicing, payment and cost allocation all handled by Brio.
