面向海外自有实体的薪酬全流程

A full payroll cycle for your own overseas entities

适用于已经在目标市场设立自有实体的出海企业。Brio 承接从变量收集、计算到支付的完整薪酬周期，按当地法定要求核算社保与税费，并由所在国注册会计师核验 Gross-to-Net，最终输出规范化的财务报表。

For companies that already have their own entity in the market. Brio runs the complete cycle — from variable collection through calculation to payment — computes social insurance and taxes to local statutory rules, has a CPA in-country verify gross-to-net, and produces standardised reporting.
