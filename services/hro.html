和 EOR 有什么不同

How this differs from EOR

与 EOR 不同，HR 代运营发生在你已经有当地实体的情况下：劳动合同仍由你的实体签署，缺的是执行这些合同的人手与流程。Brio 承接日常人事运营，你保留对实体与人员的最终控制权。

Unlike EOR, HRO applies when you already have a local entity: your entity still signs the contracts — what is missing is the people and process to run them. Brio takes on day-to-day HR operations while you keep ultimate control of the entity and the team.
