What is EOR

什么是名义雇主 EOR

What Employer of Record means

出海企业在无当地实体情况下快速雇佣员工的常见形式：企业外包部分雇主责任，同时保留对员工的管理权。由当地名义雇主提供劳动合同处理、薪酬计算发放与 HR 相关支持。

The standard way to hire quickly in a market where you have no local entity: you outsource part of the employer obligations while retaining management control of the employee. The local employer of record handles contracts, payroll calculation and payment, and HR support.

EOR 为出海企业降低了三道门槛

Three barriers EOR removes

01

无需自建实体

No local entity needed

不必承担建立自有实体的高昂成本与繁杂手续，绕过市场准入的长周期。

Avoid the high cost and paperwork of setting up your own entity, and skip the long market-entry cycle.

02

无需担忧当地合规

No local compliance burden

由当地持牌雇主承担劳动法合规责任，企业不必自行研读各国法规。

The licensed local employer carries labor-law compliance, so you don't have to master every country's regulations.

03

无需分心雇佣细节

No employment admin

从合同、算薪、社保到假勤报销，交由平台与本地团队处理，专注业务拓展。

Contracts, payroll, social insurance, leave and expenses are handled by the platform and local teams — you focus on growth.
