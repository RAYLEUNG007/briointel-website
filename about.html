我们做什么

What we do

Brio 聚焦出海企业的全球用工管理，覆盖本地员工、外派员工、代雇（EOR）员工与独立顾问（IC）四类人群，由 Payroll Platform 参与其入转调离全流程管控，并提供人员管理、薪酬处理与可视化 BI。

Brio focuses on global employment management for companies expanding overseas, covering four populations — local employees, assignees, EOR employees and independent contractors. Our payroll platform takes part in the full hire-to-exit cycle and provides people management, payroll processing and visual BI.
